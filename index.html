About me

From the family Windows 98 PC to an MSP helpdesk.

I've been hands-on with computers since the family Windows 98 PC. I studied Computer Science at GCSE, and after sixth form I went into a full-time IT role, completing my apprenticeship alongside it. Today I'm a Tier 2 Service Desk Analyst at a managed service provider, supporting Microsoft 365, identity, devices, security tools, networks and business applications for companies of all kinds.

I learn by doing. During my apprenticeship I worked 40-hour weeks in a role with no coding, so I taught myself C# in the evenings and built my apprenticeship project in 24 hours. Years later I rebuilt it as a secure web app using what I'd learned since. I'm studying for MS-102 the same way: hands-on, in my own Microsoft 365 test tenant.

I'm easy to work with and happy to help. I like getting to the root of a problem, explaining the fix in plain English, and using PowerShell to make repeat jobs faster for the whole team.

I also have a knack for software nobody else has seen before. Give me an unfamiliar application, whether it's accountancy software, an ID card system, a printer or a niche industry tool, and I'll work through the documentation until I find the fix.

My favourite thing to hear from a user:Thanks! That worked.
